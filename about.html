02 OUR STORY

FROM A SMALL IDEA TO A MOVEMENT.

EST. 2014

NOVA FITNESS started with one simple belief: your environment can change the way you train.

We built NOVA for people who wanted more than crowded floors and generic workout plans. Our goal was to create a focused environment where coaching, community and performance could exist under one roof.

Today, thousands of members train with us every week. But the philosophy hasn't changed: show up, train with purpose and keep improving.
